# 5B Structural Location

## 1. INPUT
- Current price and confirmed structural levels from Engine 3.

## 2. PROCESSING
- Map price to structural areas such as swing boundaries, internal/external levels and relevant zones.

## 3. OUTPUT
- Location classification, nearest structural references, distance metrics and confidence.

## 4. GATE
- FAIL when required structural levels are unconfirmed or unavailable.

## 5. SCORE
- 0-100 location-quality score based on level significance and measurement clarity.

## 6. TRACEABILITY
- Store level IDs, distance method, timestamp, output and reason codes.
