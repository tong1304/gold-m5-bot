# 4C — REACTION / REJECTION

## 1. INPUT
Liquidity zones/events, closed-candle price behavior.

## 2. PROCESSING
Measure observable reaction away from a zone and classify rejection strength without defining entry.

## 3. OUTPUT
Reaction state, magnitude/persistence evidence, confidence, reasons.

## 4. GATE
BLOCK when zone or reaction evidence is invalid.

## 5. SCORE
Reaction quality 0–100; evidence only.

## 6. TRACEABILITY
Record zone ID, candles, measurements, timestamp, score, gate.

**DECISION BOUNDARY:** reaction analysis only.
