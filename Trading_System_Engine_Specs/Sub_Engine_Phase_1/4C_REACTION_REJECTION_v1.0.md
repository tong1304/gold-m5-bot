# 4C Reaction / Rejection

## 1. INPUT
- Liquidity-zone or structural level reference plus closed OHLC reaction candles.

## 2. PROCESSING
- Measure rejection/reaction behavior after contact, including close location and follow-through evidence available at evaluation time.

## 3. OUTPUT
- Reaction state, rejection evidence, quality and confidence.

## 4. GATE
- FAIL when the referenced level or reaction candle is invalid.

## 5. SCORE
- 0-100 reaction-quality score from rejection magnitude, close behavior and confirmation.

## 6. TRACEABILITY
- Record level ID, candle IDs, measurement method, score and reason codes.
