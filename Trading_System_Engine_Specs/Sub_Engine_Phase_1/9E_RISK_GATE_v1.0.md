# 9E Risk Gate

## 1. INPUT
- Complete risk-engine outputs including invalidation, stop, target/R, sizing and exposure limits.

## 2. PROCESSING
- Verify that the proposed risk package is internally consistent and compliant.

## 3. OUTPUT
- Risk gate status, risk package reference, blocking conditions and confidence.

## 4. GATE
- FAIL on any hard risk violation or missing mandatory risk component.

## 5. SCORE
- 0-100 risk-readiness score for diagnostics only.

## 6. TRACEABILITY
- Record risk component IDs/versions, timestamp, policy version and reason codes.
